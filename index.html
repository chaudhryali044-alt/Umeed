Our Story

Because Hope Should Be Everyone's Right.

Umeed Foundation Layyah is a non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting underprivileged communities in Layyah and surrounding areas of Punjab, Pakistan.

Founded by 2 childhood best friends, the foundation works tirelessly to provide food, education, healthcare support, and emergency relief to those in need.

Our mission is to bring hope — 'Umeed' — to every corner of our community. We believe that everyone deserves access to basic necessities, education, and opportunities for a better future.

Mission & Vision

Why We Exist

Umeed is a Layyah based organisation with particular focus on providing quality educational and developmental services to the most vulnerable members of our society.

We envision a Punjab where no child goes to bed hungry, where every child has access to quality education, and where communities are empowered to uplift themselves.

Our Work

Six Pillars of Hope

Every pillar represents a basic human right. We fight every day to ensure communities of Layyah have access to all six.

🍱Pillar 01

Food

Hunger is a daily reality for too many families in Layyah. We organize regular food distribution drives, providing nutritious ration packages. During Ramadan and emergencies, we scale up to ensure no family goes without a meal.

🏫Pillar 02

Education

Education is the most powerful tool for breaking the cycle of poverty. We support local schools with stationery, furniture and programmes designed to inspire children to dream big and reach their full potential.

👕Pillar 03

Clothes

A uniform gives a child confidence and belonging. We distribute school uniforms and winter clothing to children who cannot afford them, restoring dignity and encouraging regular school attendance.

🏥Pillar 04

Healthcare

With guidance from our co-founder Arham Khan Tangwani, a medical professional, we facilitate healthcare access for underprivileged community members through medical camps and health awareness programmes.

🏠Pillar 05

Shelter

We work to address housing challenges faced by vulnerable families, connecting them with shelter support during emergencies and advocating for safe, dignified living conditions.

🛡️Pillar 06

Safety

Creating safe environments for children and women is central to our mission. We build community awareness, provide crisis support and ensure children can grow up free from harm.
